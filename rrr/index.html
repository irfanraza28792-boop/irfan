The Story

Once, while scrolling through Instagram, I noticed an account named “_jahan____e_n” in my suggestions.

I opened her profile just out of curiosity. There were a few pictures, but one of them made me stop.

She was beautiful—especially her eyes. She was wearing a blue dress, with one hand resting on her left ear and red Mehendi on her hand.

I don't know what it was, but something about that picture stayed with me. I kept looking at it again and again.

There were so many suggested accounts, but somehow, hers felt different.

I thought about messaging her, but then I wondered, “Would she even reply?”

Still, I sent.

“Hi, what's up?”

It was around 1:30 AM.

After five or ten minutes, a notification appeared. It was her reply.

That little reply made me smile.

I didn't really know how to continue the conversation, and my phone had only 3% battery, so I eventually put it away.

I didn't know that this small conversation at 1:30 AM would slowly become something so close to my heart.

She was completely different from me.

She was an extrovert—always in a chilling mood, full of energy, fun, and entertainment. She had a way of making even ordinary moments feel enjoyable.

And she was so naturally gorgeous that sometimes I genuinely had no words to describe her behaviour, her personality, or the way she carried herself.

I, on the other hand, was an introvert guy.

But somehow, she changed me.

She made me more open, more playful, and somehow taught me how to flirt without even saying much.

And then she gave me a name: “Saheli.”

In Bangla, Saheli means a female friend. The irony of a guy being called Saheli by her somehow made the name even more special.

“Mughal ka Chomu Badshah.”

She was trolling me with it. It sounds ridiculous to me, but it became one of the most affectionate names she ever gave me.

Somehow, coming from her, even that silly name felt special.

But somewhere along the way, things became deeper than just names and conversations.

Now, she is not simply a girl to me.

She has become a feeling.

A feeling connected somewhere deep within my heart.

Among all my friends, she somehow became the person I would always choose as my first choice. Not because I had to, but because my heart naturally preferred her presence.

Our personalities were different. Our ways of thinking were different. I was quiet; she was full of life. I stayed inside my own world; she somehow pulled me out of it.

And perhaps that is what made our bond so special.

That was a wonderful journey.

But now, I am watching it slowly break apart.

Becuse of me I will never want to end...
